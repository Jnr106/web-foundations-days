About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture ideas, reminders, and everyday tasks in one place. It provides a simple space to organise notes so you can remember what matters.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your note into the New note text box.
  3. Click Add Note to submit the form.

Features

  • A clearly labelled text box for entering a note.
  • A notes area with two example notes.
  • Home and About navigation links.
  • Keyboard-accessible links and form controls.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Tab Move to the next link or form control.
Shift + Tab Move to the previous link or form control.
Enter Submit the note form when the note input is focused.
